The book has been out for a while, but fascinating parallels with the current big tech shenanigans &mdash; remember when Sam Altman got sacked from what was then the most important AI company? Is this a rite of passage for playing at the frontier? The stakes are too high, we must bring him back, etc.

Anyway, wonderful book and like everything in big tech it is mostly about power. For all the bullshit claims about safety, it's the same winner-takes-all playbook as every other VC-backed software firm. So many of the warnings in this book seem to be coming to pass, moving fast and breaking things with increasingly powerful tools.

The author is an engineer, worked in start-ups and is an incredible journalist. Whatever your P(doom), the warnings of the potential damage to democracy are as scary as the machines taking over the world.
