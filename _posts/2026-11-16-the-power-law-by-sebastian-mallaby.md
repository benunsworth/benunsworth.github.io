If nothing else, I learned that it used to be called ‘adventure capital’. 
I knew the names Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins, Andreesson Horowitz, Founders Fund and SoftBank - but never really understood how they became the dominant force in the tech industry.
The Power Law is the story of the Pareto principle and how it applies to venture backed firms. It helped me understand how the weak corporate governance of the most powerful companies in the world is a feature of the aggressive ‘founder friendly’ model that took hold on the West Coast.
The book tells the history of VC financing and covers the early internet years up until today. All your favourite big tech personalities feature along the way.
